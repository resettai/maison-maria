Products

Are MARIA products vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes. No ingredient is of animal origin, and animal testing of cosmetics and their ingredients has been banned in the EU since 2013. We do not sell in markets that require animal testing.

Are the sunscreens suitable during pregnancy?

Mineral Fluid SPF 50 uses zinc oxide only and is the one most often recommended during pregnancy. The chemical filters in the other fluids are approved by the EU Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety; many midwives still prefer mineral filters, so we make one. Night Cream uses bakuchiol instead of retinol for the same reason.

From what age can children use them?

Sport Stick, Body Fluid, After Sun and Sun Dots from 3 years; The First Routine from the first breakouts. For babies under one year, shade and clothing come first; ask your paediatrician.

Are the filters reef-safe?

MARIA contains none of the filters restricted in Hawaii, Palau or the marine reserves of the Caribbean: no oxybenzone, no octinoxate, no octocrylene. No sunscreen is entirely without footprint, which is why we also make a stick and a mist that use less product per application.

Why is there no fragrance?

Fragrance is the most common cause of cosmetic allergy, and a product you wear every day on your face should not carry that risk. The fluids smell faintly of their ingredients and of nothing else.

How long do the products keep?

Twelve months after opening for everything except Antioxidant Serum, which keeps six. Unopened, 30 months. Write the opening month on the base.

Which shade of Tinted Fluid should I choose?

Light for fair to light skin, Medium for light to olive, Deep for tan to deep. The coverage is sheer and forgiving; two shades can be mixed.

Teens and parents

I am 14. Where do I start?

Three steps: Clear Cleanser, Gel Moisturiser, Matte Fluid SPF 50+. Add Blemish Patches for the days something shows up. That is The First Routine, and it is enough. Serums, acids and night creams can wait years.

Will sunscreen make my acne worse?

Not an oil-free, non-comedogenic one. Matte Fluid is tested on acne-prone skin, and daily SPF is what stops the dark marks acne leaves behind.

Do the patches actually work?

On a blemish that has come to a head, a hydrocolloid patch draws it out overnight and stops picking, which is what causes scars. On deep spots use Blemish Gel first.

What should a parent buy first?

The First Routine if the skin is oily or breaking out, The Minis if it is not. Both are under €120 and neither contains anything a dermatologist would advise against for a teenager.

Is it suitable for boys?

Everything is unscented and unfussy. Matte Fluid and Sport Stick are the two products most often chosen by boys who have refused every other sunscreen.

Vanity Collection and the Noon light

What is the Noon light?

A frosted-glass ring light or bar built into the mirror of every Vanity case and The Compact, set to 5,000 kelvin, the colour of midday daylight, at a colour rendering index of 97. It shows skin, tint and sunscreen the way the sun will.

Does the light emit UV?

No. It is ordinary visible light from LEDs, certified as a low-voltage electrical product. It does not affect the skin or the formulas.

How is it charged, and how long does it last?

USB-C on every piece. The King Size runs about 30 hours per charge, the Medium 20 and The Compact 10. All switch off automatically.

Can I fly with it?

Yes. The batteries are far below the 100 Wh limit for hand luggage.

What if the light fails?

Two-year warranty, and the batteries are replaceable by us for a flat fee for as long as we make the product. Nothing is glued shut.

Returns

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened sunscreens cannot be resold, so we ask that you try a shade or texture carefully; if a product does not suit your skin, write to us and we will find a solution.

What if a product arrives damaged?

Send a photo within 7 days and we replace it at no cost.

The house

Where are the products made?

Formulated and filled at a family-owned manufacturer outside Lyon, France, certified to ISO 22716. The glass is French; the boxes are printed in Europe on FSC board.

Is MARIA tested in real conditions?

Yes. In addition to the ISO tests at independent laboratories, every formula goes through 28-day use panels in three climates: a northern spring, a Mediterranean summer and an alpine winter.

Do you offer refills?

Refill cartridges for Daily Fluid, Daily Moisturiser and Night Cream are planned for the second year. The glass is designed to be kept.
