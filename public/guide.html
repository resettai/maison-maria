The UV index around the world.

The teaspoon rule.

The numbers

What SPF actually measures.

SPF describes protection against UVB, the wavelengths that burn, at the tested dose. The UVA circle describes the rest.

UVA

UVA penetrates deeper, drives most visible ageing and contributes to melanoma risk. In the EU, the UVA-in-a-circle mark means UVA protection is at least a third of the SPF and the critical wavelength is 370 nm or more. All MARIA sunscreens carry it.

Water resistance

"Water resistant" keeps at least half its SPF after two 20-minute swims; "very water resistant" after four. Towelling removes more than water. Reapply after both.

Snow and altitude

Fresh snow reflects up to 80 percent of UV back at the face, and UV rises roughly 10 percent per 1,000 metres. A March day on the mountain can match a June day in the city.

The routine

Morning and evening, in order.

Morning

  1. Cleanser, or water if the skin is dry
  2. Antioxidant Serum, four drops, wait a minute
  3. Hydrating Serum in the dry months
  4. Daily Moisturiser for normal to dry skin
  5. Daily, Tinted or Mineral Fluid, two pumps, last
  6. Lip SPF 30 and Hand Cream SPF 30 on the way out

Midday and evening

  1. Sun Mist over make-up every two hours outdoors, or Sport Stick on bare skin
  2. Cleanser, twice after a day of reapplying
  3. Exfoliating Toner three evenings a week
  4. Hydrating Serum
  5. Night Cream, or After Sun after a long day outside
