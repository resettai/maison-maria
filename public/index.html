Testing and standards

What every MARIA sunscreen goes through.

  • SPF in vivoISO 24444 at an independent laboratory. 50+ means a measured SPF of at least 60.
  • UVA at least a third of SPFISO 24443, critical wavelength above 370 nm. The UVA circle on every sunscreen.
  • Water resistanceISO 16217: 40 minutes for the face fluids, 80 minutes for Sport Stick and Body Fluid.
  • Notified before saleSafety assessment, Product Information File and CPNP notification under EU Regulation 1223/2009.
