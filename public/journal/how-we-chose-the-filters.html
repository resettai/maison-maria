How we chose the filters

Europe approves around thirty UV filters. MARIA uses four of them, plus zinc oxide for the mineral fluid. Here is what we left out and why.

October 2026 · 6 min read

A UV filter is a molecule that absorbs or scatters ultraviolet light before it reaches living skin. The European Union keeps a positive list of the filters a sunscreen may contain, with maximum concentrations, and reviews it as the science moves. It is the strictest list in the world, and it is the reason the newest filters have been available in Europe for two decades while American sunscreens still rely on chemistry from the 1970s.

The four we use

Tinosorb S (Bis-Ethylhexyloxyphenol Methoxyphenyl Triazine) and Tinosorb M (Methylene Bis-Benzotriazolyl Tetramethylbutylphenol) are broad-spectrum filters that cover UVB and the full UVA range, do not break down in sunlight and stabilise the filters around them. They are large molecules that stay on the surface rather than being absorbed. Uvinul A Plus (Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate) is a dedicated long-UVA filter that keeps the UVA ratio where the EU circle requires it. Uvinul T150 (Ethylhexyl Triazone) is one of the most efficient UVB filters per gram, which is how a fluid reaches SPF 50+ while staying light.

The ones we left out

Octocrylene can degrade into benzophenone, a suspected carcinogen, as a product ages; it is also a frequent cause of contact allergy. Homosalate has had its permitted concentration cut by the EU over endocrine concerns, which makes a 50+ formula built on it hard to justify. Benzophenone-3 (oxybenzone) is a known allergen, is absorbed through the skin and is restricted or banned in several marine reserves. Octinoxate is photo-unstable and shares the marine restrictions. None of these is needed to reach 50+ with modern chemistry, so none of them is in MARIA.

Why a mineral fluid as well

Zinc oxide is the filter with the longest tolerance record, which makes it the choice for reactive skin, for pregnancy and for the months after laser or peels. On its own it reaches SPF 50 rather than 50+, and it always leaves some tint, which is why Mineral Fluid carries a whisper of iron oxides. We use non-nano zinc oxide at 22 percent.

What this means for the texture

Because the four filters are efficient, the total filter load in Daily Fluid is lower than in most 50+ products on the shelf. That is where the weightlessness comes from. It is also why the fluid can be fragrance-free: there is less to mask.
