Why France

A new brand could be made anywhere. The sunscreen expertise, the glass and the regulatory culture made the choice for us.

October 2026 · 4 min read

A new brand could be made anywhere. France produces more sunscreen than any other country in Europe, and has done since the category existed. The laboratories that formulate for the big houses are mostly within two hours of Lyon, and so are the contract manufacturers that produce for brands without factories of their own. That is where MARIA is made.

What the factory brings

Our manufacturing partner has made skincare and sun care since 1947. It runs its own SPF laboratory, so a batch is measured before it leaves the building rather than weeks later. The site is certified to ISO 22716, the good-manufacturing-practice standard for cosmetics, and approved for COSMOS natural and organic certification. Every MARIA sunscreen is also tested at independent laboratories: SPF in vivo to ISO 24444, UVA in vitro to ISO 24443 and water resistance to ISO 16217.

The regulatory culture

Under EU Regulation 1223/2009, every cosmetic product needs a Product Information File, a safety assessment by a qualified assessor and a notification in the Cosmetic Products Notification Portal before it can be sold. French manufacturers have been working inside that framework for longer than anyone, and it shows in the documentation that arrives with a batch.

The glass

MARIA fluids come in frosted glass with airless pumps, which keeps air and light away from the filters and the vitamin C. The glass is made in France too, from a glassworks that supplies the perfume houses, and it is heavier than plastic in the hand on purpose.

And the rest of the world

The formulas, the glass and the filling are French. The use panels run in three climates, the design is European and the products are sold wherever there is daylight. Every bottle says Fabriqué en France on the base.
