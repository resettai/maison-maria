Maison Maria

A sun-care house, made in France.

MARIA is built around one idea: that protection should be the easiest step in the routine, not the one you skip. Sun care formulated like skincare, skincare formulated around the sun, and no claims we cannot document.

Principles

Six things we hold to.

  1. The highest category, every day

    Every face fluid is 50+, the top of the EU scale, with UVA protection above the legal minimum. The number is the same on the lightest texture and the tinted one.

  2. Filters chosen, not inherited

    Four modern photostable filters and zinc oxide. No octocrylene, homosalate, oxybenzone or octinoxate, because 50+ does not need them.

  3. Nothing that does not earn its place

    No fragrance, no colourants beyond the iron oxides that give a tint, no alcohol that dries. If an ingredient is in the bottle, it is on the product page with its reason.

  4. Tested where it will be worn

    ISO tests at independent laboratories in France, and 28-day use panels in three climates: a northern spring, a Mediterranean summer and an alpine winter.

  5. Made to be kept

    Frosted glass, airless pumps, refills planned from the second year. Boxes in FSC board, printed in Europe. Vegan throughout.

  6. Light you can trust

    Every Vanity case opens to a mirror lit to midday daylight, 5,000 K at CRI 97. Skincare and SPF are checked in the light they will be seen in, not under a bathroom bulb.

Testing and standards

How a MARIA sunscreen is proven.

Every sunscreen is tested at independent laboratories and notified in the EU before it is sold. These are the methods behind the numbers on the label.

  • SPF in vivoISO 24444 at an independent laboratory. 50+ means a measured SPF of at least 60.
  • UVA at least a third of SPFISO 24443, critical wavelength above 370 nm. The UVA circle on every sunscreen.
  • Water resistanceISO 16217: 40 minutes for the face fluids, 80 minutes for Sport Stick and Body Fluid.
  • Notified before saleSafety assessment, Product Information File and CPNP notification under EU Regulation 1223/2009.

The regulatory frame

Cosmetics sold in Europe are governed by Regulation (EC) 1223/2009, the strictest cosmetics framework in the world, and MARIA is made to it wherever it is sold. Each product has a Product Information File, a Cosmetic Product Safety Report signed by a qualified assessor, and a Responsible Person who answers for it. UV filters may only be those on the regulation's positive list, at or below the permitted concentration. Sunscreen labelling follows Commission Recommendation 2006/647/EC: eight SPF categories, the UVA circle, and no claims of total or all-day protection.

Skincare too

The products without SPF go through stability testing at 25 and 40 degrees, challenge testing of the preservative system, dermatological testing under occlusion, and for the barrier products a 28-day measurement of transepidermal water loss. Results are summarised on each product page and are provisional until the final formulas are approved.

Packaging

Glass, board and what we left out.

Glass

Fluids, serums and creams come in frosted glass with airless pumps or wide-mouth jars. Glass is heavier to ship than plastic and better for the filters, the vitamin C and the hand. Refill cartridges for the three most used products are planned for year two.

Board

Outer boxes are FSC-certified ivory board, printed in Europe with blind embossing instead of foil. There is no cellophane, no insert and no leaflet; the product page is the leaflet.

Leather, without the animal

The Vanity Collection is made from a solvent-free polyurethane on recycled polyester, chosen for durability over the cheaper options. Hardware is plated zinc alloy, zips are YKK.
