Privacy.

Maison Maria (MARIA Skincare AS, in formation, Ålesund, Norway) is the data controller for personal data collected on this site.

What we collect

On this preview site, nothing is collected or stored by us. The waitlist form does not send data. The cart is kept in your own browser only. The password you enter to open the site is checked and not stored; a cookie remembers that you have access for 30 days and contains no personal data.

When the shop opens

We will collect the data needed to fulfil an order: name, address, email, phone number for the carrier, and payment confirmation from the payment provider. We will not store card numbers. Order data is kept for five years as required by Norwegian accounting law. Marketing emails are sent only with consent and can be stopped from any email.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your data at any time by writing to privacy@maisonmaria.no. Complaints can be made to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

Cookies

This site sets one cookie, for access to the preview. It sets no analytics or advertising cookies.
