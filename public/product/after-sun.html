How to use

Apply generously to clean skin in the evening after sun exposure, face and body. Repeat in the morning if the skin feels tight. Keep in the fridge in summer.

Questions

Does it treat sunburn?

It calms and rehydrates skin that has had too much sun. A burn with blisters, fever or large areas needs medical advice, not a balm.

Can I use it as a daily moisturiser?

On the body, yes. On the face, Daily Moisturiser is formulated to sit under SPF and make-up.
