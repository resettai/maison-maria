Questions

It tingles slightly. Is that normal?

A short warming or tingling on application is normal at pH 3.2. If it stings or stays red, use it every other morning for two weeks.

Why 10 percent and not 20?

Ten percent gives most of the benefit with far less irritation. With ferulic acid and vitamin E the photoprotection is already at its peak.

What if it turns orange?

A faint straw colour is normal. Deep orange means the vitamin C has oxidised; replace the bottle.
