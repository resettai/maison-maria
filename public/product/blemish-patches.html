How to use

On clean, dry skin, press a patch over the blemish and leave it for at least six hours or overnight. The patch turns white as it absorbs. Replace when it does. Apply skincare around, not over, the patch.

Questions

Do they work on deep spots?

Patches work best on blemishes that have come to a head. For deep, painful spots use Blemish Gel and give it time; a patch on top stops picking.

Can I wear them to school?

The Day patches are made for exactly that: matte, thin and invisible under a little Tinted Fluid.
