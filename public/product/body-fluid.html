How to use

Apply generously 15 minutes before sun: about six pumps for arms, shoulders and décolleté, more for legs. Reapply every two hours and after swimming or towelling.

Questions

How much do I need for a whole body?

Around 30 ml, a fifth of the bottle, for an adult in swimwear. Most people use a third of that, which is why sunburn happens through sunscreen.

Can I use it on my face?

You can, but the face fluids are lighter and formulated for pores and make-up.
