How to use

Massage one pump in dry hands over a dry face for 30 seconds. Add water until the gel turns to milk, rinse off. Use morning and evening; after a day of reapplied SPF, cleanse twice.

Questions

Do I need a double cleanse?

After one morning application, a single cleanse is enough. After a day of reapplying water-resistant SPF, cleanse twice.

Does it remove eye make-up?

Light mascara and shadow, yes. For waterproof mascara use a dedicated remover first.
