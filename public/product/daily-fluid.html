How to use

Apply generously as the last step of your morning routine, 15 minutes before going out. Two full pumps cover face, neck and ears. Reapply every two hours outdoors, and after sweating or swimming.

Questions

Can I wear it under foundation?

Yes. Let it settle for a minute, then apply make-up as usual. It leaves no white cast and no greasy film.

How much should I use?

Two full pumps for face, neck and ears. SPF is tested at 2 mg per square centimetre; using half the amount gives far less than half the protection.

Do I need to reapply if I work indoors?

Indoors behind glass, one morning application is usually enough. Outdoors, reapply every two hours and after sweating or swimming.
