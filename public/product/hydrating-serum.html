How to use

Press three drops into slightly damp skin, morning or evening, before cream or SPF. In winter, use it both.

Questions

Can I use it with the Antioxidant Serum?

Yes. Vitamin C first, wait a minute, then Hydrating Serum, then SPF. Or use vitamin C in the morning and this one at night.

Does it pill under SPF?

No. It is water-light and absorbs in under a minute. Let it settle before the fluid.
