Questions

Why SPF 30 and not 50?

A lip balm is reapplied many times a day, and SPF 30 already blocks about 97 percent of UVB. Higher filter loads make a balm waxy and bitter.

Can it help against cold sores?

UV exposure is a common trigger for cold sores. Daily SPF on the lips reduces that trigger; it does not treat an outbreak.
