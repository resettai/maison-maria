Questions

Will sunscreen make my spots worse?

An oil-free, non-comedogenic SPF will not, and sun exposure makes post-acne marks darker and last longer. Matte Fluid is tested on acne-prone skin for that reason.

Can I use it with Blemish Gel?

Yes. Blemish Gel on spots first, let it dry, then Matte Fluid over the whole face.
