Questions

Do I need it under Daily Fluid?

Oily skin often does not. Normal to dry skin, and anyone in winter, will find the fluid sits better over it.

Is it rich enough for winter?

For most people, yes. For very dry skin, layer Hydrating Serum underneath or use Night Cream in the evening.
