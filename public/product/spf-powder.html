How to use

Twist the base until powder reaches the brush, tap off the excess and dust over face in circles until the skin is evenly covered. Use generously: a light dusting is a finish, a full pass is protection. Reapply every two hours outdoors.

Questions

Is a powder enough on its own?

No. Powder is for topping up a morning SPF. Most people apply far too little powder for the tested SPF, so start the day with a fluid.
