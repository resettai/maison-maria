How to use

Stick a dot on clean, dry skin on the back of the hand. Apply sunscreen over the dot and the hand as usual. When the dot turns terracotta, reapply on the dot and on yourself. One dot lasts a day.

Questions

Is it accurate?

It is a reminder, not a dosimeter. The dot is calibrated to change colour roughly when a normal application of SPF 30–50 has stopped working. Reapply earlier after swimming.

Can children use them?

Yes, from 3 years. They are the easiest way to make reapplication a game rather than an argument.
