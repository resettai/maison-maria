How to use

Close eyes and mouth, hold 15 cm from the face and spray in a slow cross pattern until the skin feels evenly damp. Let dry. Reapply every two hours outdoors.

Questions

Is a mist enough on its own?

Sun Mist is made for topping up a morning SPF. Used alone, most people spray too little to reach the tested protection. Start the day with Daily or Tinted Fluid.

Will it move my make-up?

No. The mist is fine enough to settle without droplets. Let it dry for a minute before touching the face.
