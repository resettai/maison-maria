How to use

Apply as the last step in the morning, alone or under powder. Blend with fingertips from the centre of the face outwards. Mix shades if needed. Reapply every two hours outdoors.

Questions

Which shade should I choose?

Light suits fair to light skin, Medium suits light to olive skin, Deep suits tan to deep skin. The coverage is sheer, so a shade slightly off still blends. Two shades can be mixed.

Does it replace foundation?

For most days, yes. It evens out tone without covering freckles. For more coverage, use it as a base under your usual foundation.
