Questions

How much more does it hold than the King Size?

Twice the volume: two tiers instead of one, 14 compartments instead of 8, and the lower tier is deep enough for 150 ml bottles standing up. The twelve products inside leave roughly a third of the space free.

Can I choose the products inside?

At launch the trunk comes pre-packed. Shade of Tinted Fluid can be chosen at checkout; from the second season the trunk can be configured.

Can it be checked in?

Yes. It ships with a dust bag and the corners are reinforced for exactly that. The battery is far below airline limits.
