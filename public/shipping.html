Shipping and returns.

Delivery

Orders ship from our warehouse outside Oslo within one working day. Delivery takes two to four working days in Norway and three to seven in the EU and the United Kingdom. You receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves.

Prices include Norwegian VAT. For EU and UK orders, local VAT and duties are calculated at checkout so that nothing is charged on delivery.

Returns

Unopened products in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Write to care@maisonmaria.no with your order number and we send a prepaid return label. Refunds are made to the original payment method within five working days of receipt.

Opened products cannot be resold and are not refundable, with one exception: if a product does not suit your skin, tell us within 14 days and we will exchange it or credit you towards another product once.

Damaged or wrong items

Send a photo of the parcel and the product within 7 days of delivery and we replace the item at no cost, without asking you to return it.

These terms apply from the opening of the shop in June 2027 and are subject to approval. Statutory rights under Norwegian and EU consumer law are not affected.
