Shipping and returns.

Delivery

Orders ship within one working day from our central European warehouse. Delivery takes two to five working days in Europe and four to eight working days elsewhere. You receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves.

Returns

Unopened products in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Write to care@maisonmaria.com with your order number and we send a prepaid return label. Refunds are made to the original payment method within five working days of receipt.

Opened products cannot be resold and are not refundable, with one exception: if a product does not suit your skin, tell us within 14 days and we will exchange it or credit you towards another product once.

Damaged or wrong items

Send a photo of the parcel and the product within 7 days of delivery and we replace the item at no cost, without asking you to return it.
