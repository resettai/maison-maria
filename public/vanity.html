Noon, in every case.

Why 5,000 K

Midday daylight sits around 5,000 kelvin. Bathroom bulbs sit at 2,700. Tint, SPF and make-up checked under one look different under the other, which is why so much looks right indoors and wrong at the door. The Noon setting shows you what the sun will see.

Why CRI 97

Colour rendering index measures how truthfully a light shows colour. Most LED bulbs score 80, which flattens skin tones and reddens nothing. The Noon lights are specified at 97, close to daylight, so a shade match made in the case holds outside it.

Built to be charged rarely

USB-C on every piece, 30 hours in the King Size, 20 in the Medium, 10 in The Compact, with auto-off. The LEDs are rated for 30,000 hours; the batteries are replaceable by us. Nothing is glued shut.

The material

The cases are made from a solvent-free polyurethane on a recycled polyester backing, embossed with a fine pebble grain and edge-painted in espresso. It was chosen over leather to keep the collection vegan, and over cheaper vegan options because it does not crack at the corners after a winter in a handbag.

Hardware is zinc alloy plated in champagne gold, zips are YKK Excella. The lining is ivory microsuede, wipe-clean, with elastic loops sized to the MARIA bottles. Every case carries a discreet gold MARIA mark on the front and Fabriqué en France on the inside label.
