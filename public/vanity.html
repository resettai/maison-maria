The material

The cases are made from a solvent-free polyurethane on a recycled polyester backing, embossed with a fine pebble grain and edge-painted in espresso. It was chosen over leather to keep the collection vegan, and over cheaper vegan options because it does not crack at the corners after a winter in a handbag.

Hardware is zinc alloy plated in champagne gold, zips are YKK Excella. The lining is ivory microsuede, wipe-clean, with elastic loops sized to the MARIA bottles. Every case carries a discreet gold MARIA mark on the front and Fabriqué en France on the inside label.

Materials, dimensions and prices are provisional until the manufacturer and payment are approved.
